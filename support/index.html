Bug, a purchase that didn't unlock, a question about a game mode — whatever it is, this is the fastest way to reach us.

Email support evolvdchaos@gmail.com

We read every message. Response times can vary, so for anything time-sensitive, mention it in the subject line.

Before you email — a couple of things that fix most issues

  • Force-quit and reopen GameNight — clears up most stuck screens or games that won't advance.
  • Check you're on the latest version from the App Store / Play Store — most fixes ship there first.
  • If a purchase didn't unlock a pack, try Store → Restore before emailing — it re-syncs your purchases from Apple/Google automatically.
