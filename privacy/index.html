Plain-English summary
  • Your player names and stats stay on your phone. We don't have a server that stores them, and we can't see them.
  • Playing online sends your display name and moves to the other players in your room, briefly — nothing is saved once the room closes.
  • No accounts, no email address, nothing sold. We don't run analytics.
  • Purchases go through RevenueCat; ads (for non-subscribers) come from Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising identifier — on iOS, only if you allow it when asked — see §4.
  • This app contains mature content in some optional packs — see §5.

01What we collect

GameNight is built to work entirely on your device. When you add a player, play a round, or check your stats, that information — player names, games played, Imposter win rate, trivia accuracy — is written to your phone's local storage (technically, AsyncStorage) and never leaves it. We don't run a server that receives it, so there's nothing for us to collect, see, or lose.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes this data completely and immediately. There is no cloud backup of it to also delete.

Two parts of the app do involve an outside company. Buying a pack or subscription is handled by Apple / Google and by RevenueCat, which we use to check what you've purchased and to unlock it across your devices. RevenueCat sees your purchase (which product, when) and a random ID it assigns to your install — never your name or email. Ads, shown only to players without a subscription, come from Google AdMob. Both are described in §4.

02Online play

Hosting or joining an online room is the one feature that talks to a server. It uses Supabase's Realtime service to relay, live, whatever a game round needs to work:

  • the display name you type in for that session,
  • your votes on which game to play,
  • drawing strokes and guesses (Draw & Guess),
  • dial positions and guesses (Wavelength).

These are sent over what Supabase calls Broadcast and Presence channels — built for exactly this kind of live, temporary relay. They are not written to a database. Once everyone leaves the room (or the room closes), that session's data isn't stored anywhere and can't be looked back up — by us or anyone else.

Honor-system note: in Draw & Guess and Wavelength, the secret word or target is currently broadcast to every device in the room, with only the relevant player's screen displaying it. It's technically possible for someone to intercept that data on their own device. We chose this trade-off deliberately to avoid needing accounts or a database for online play — treat online rooms as you would a shared game with people you trust in the room.

04Third parties

Three outside services are involved, each for one specific job. We don't share your local player data or stats with any of them.

ServiceUsed forWhat it processes
Supabase Relaying online rooms (§2) Your session display name, votes, and moves, live only — not written to a database, gone when the room closes.
RevenueCat Managing purchases Which pack/subscription you bought and when, plus a random install ID. No name, email, or contact details. Linked to your App Store / Google account by the store, not by us.
Google AdMob Showing ads to non-subscribers Device and usage signals needed to serve and measure ads, and — subject to your consent (iOS App Tracking Transparency, and the EU/UK consent form) — an advertising identifier. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

A subscription removes ads entirely, so subscribers' devices make no ad requests.

05Age & content

GameNight is a party game app not directed at children. Some optional content is intended for adults:

  • Optional premium packs across several modes (marked "After Dark" or "Extreme" in-app) contain mature, suggestive, or crude content, clearly labeled with a content warning before you can select them.

We don't knowingly collect any information from children, and the app's own age rating on the App Store / Play Store reflects this content — please respect it.

06Keeping & deleting data

  • Local data (players, stats, settings, owned packs): kept until you clear it yourself, by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. We have no copy and can't delete it for you remotely — there's nothing on our side to delete.
  • Online room data: exists only while the room is open, and is gone the moment it closes. There's no retention period to describe because nothing is retained.

07Your choices

  • Personalised ads: decline the iOS "Allow tracking?" prompt, or the EU/UK consent form, and AdMob serves non-personalised ads instead. You can change the iOS choice any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • No ads at all: a GameNight Premium subscription removes them.
  • Local data: clear it whenever you like by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage (§6).
